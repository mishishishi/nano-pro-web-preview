Clinicinterior

Town, country · 2022 · Healthcare · 1,200 m²

A waiting room, two corridors and the treatment rooms off them, coated over a weekend and back in use on the Monday.

This entry is a sample, written the way a real one will read. The place, the photographs, the quote and everything described here are invented to show the shape of an entry — no such project, and no such result, is being claimed.

What wedid here

The waiting room filled from eight in the morning and had one window onto the street. Staff aired it between appointments, and through the winter it stayed shut.

Ceilings and upper walls were coated over a weekend — the waiting room, both corridors and four treatment rooms — with the practice closed on the Saturday and open again on the Monday.

Staff describe the rooms as fresher by the end of the day, and the door is no longer propped open through the winter.
